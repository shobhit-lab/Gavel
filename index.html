Features

01

Incident Triggers

Gavel turns every email, web form, and ticket into a structured incident on arrival. Sender, content, attachments, and linked order or account are captured automatically. Filters by keyword, sender, or priority keep noise out and open rooms only for relevant signals.

Each trigger opens a dedicated room holding people, agents, evidence, and draft actions. The whole team sees activity in real time, and late joiners get the full prior history. The room closes when the incident is resolved and the record is saved.

Classifies severity within moments using content, customer, and issue type. Recommends an owner and response speed; the team can override any call. Uncertain cases are flagged for human confirmation, and urgent issues reach a person first.

Pulls order history, shipment status, prior conversations, and related incidents from connected tools. Delivers a sourced brief within minutes instead of an hour of tab-hunting. Never contacts customers; it only informs the decision.

Drafts the fix: customer reply, refund, status update, or internal task. Every draft cites its evidence and is never sent automatically. Reviewers see the draft beside the research brief.
